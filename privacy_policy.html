Effective date: 29 May 2026

CastDrop is a local Wi-Fi file transfer app for Android phones and Android TV. It does not require an account, does not run advertisements, and does not use third-party analytics. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles, why, and how long it keeps it.

Package identifier: app.localshare.android

Transfers are not encrypted in transit. Files are sent over your local Wi-Fi network without end-to-end encryption. Only use CastDrop on networks you trust. Data never leaves your local network (except anonymised crash reports — see Section 5).

Section 2

How we use your data

CastDrop uses data only for the purposes listed in Section 1. We do not:

No advertising or profiling

Your data is never used to show ads or build a profile of your behaviour.

No sale or sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party except Sentry for crash reporting (Section 5).

No remote storage of files

File contents are streamed directly between devices over Wi-Fi. They pass through no server we operate.

Section 3

How long we keep your data

Device UUID

Kept until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

Transfer history

Kept on your device until you clear it from within the app or uninstall. You can delete individual entries at any time.

Crash reports

Sentry retains crash data for 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted from their servers.

Camera and network data

Processed in memory only; discarded as soon as the relevant operation completes. Nothing is written to storage.

Section 4

Android permissions explained

CAMERA

Used exclusively for QR code scanning during device pairing. The app requests this permission only when you initiate a QR scan and releases it immediately after.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows the app to start a background service after the device boots so you can receive files without opening the app first. You can disable this in the app's settings.

INTERNET / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE

Required to send and receive files over Wi-Fi and to broadcast or receive UDP discovery packets on the local network.

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Used to read files you choose to send and to save files you receive to the folder you have selected. We never access files outside of the folders you designate.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Keeps a transfer in progress even if you switch to another app, preventing interrupted transfers.

Section 5

Third-party services

CastDrop uses one third-party service:

Sentry — crash reporting

When the app crashes, an anonymised report is sent to Sentry containing: Android OS version, app version, and the state of any in-progress transfer (e.g. bytes transferred, transfer direction). No file names, file contents, IP addresses, or device identifiers are included in crash reports.

Sentry is operated by Functional Software, Inc. Their privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

No advertising SDK, analytics platform, or other third-party library has access to your data.

Section 6

Security

CastDrop transfers files directly over your local Wi-Fi network. Transfers are not encrypted in transit. This means that other devices on the same network could, in principle, intercept the data being transferred. We therefore strongly recommend using CastDrop only on private, trusted networks — not on public Wi-Fi.

Data stored on your device (transfer history, device UUID) is protected by Android's standard application sandboxing and is not accessible to other apps.

Section 7

Children's privacy

CastDrop is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the app requires no account or registration and stores no identifying personal information on any server, it does not present specific risks for younger users beyond those described in Section 6.

Section 8

Your rights and controls

Delete transfer history

You can clear your transfer history at any time from within the app. Individual entries can also be deleted one by one.

Reset device UUID

Clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → CastDrop → Clear Data) generates a new UUID and removes all locally stored data.

Disable boot autostart

You can turn off the boot-time background receiver in the app's settings. This prevents CastDrop from starting automatically after a reboot.

Opt out of crash reporting

You can disable crash reporting in the app's settings. No crash data will be sent to Sentry while this option is off.

Revoke permissions

You may revoke Camera or Storage permissions at any time via Android Settings. Revoking a permission disables the feature that depends on it.
